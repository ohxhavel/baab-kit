# registry/

The business-entity registry. One folder per legal or operating entity, each with a
1-pager (`_index.md`) that acts as the entity's "book cover".

- This business itself lives at `{{slug}}/`.
- Spawn a new entity with `baab new entity <slug>` — never create one by hand.
- `_registry.md` is generated by `baab index`; edit the entity files, not the table.

An entity 1-pager captures identity, ownership, and the accounts the entity runs on.
Keep secrets as `op://vault/item/field` references only.
