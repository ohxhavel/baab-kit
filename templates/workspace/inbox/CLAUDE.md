# inbox/

A single place to drop unsorted notes before they're routed into the tree. Notes
here are **exempt from frontmatter and naming rules** — capture first, organize
later.

When a note here has found its home, add frontmatter and move it into the right
folder (or spawn a proper entity/project/client/app for it with `baab new`). Don't
let the inbox become a second home for facts that belong elsewhere.
